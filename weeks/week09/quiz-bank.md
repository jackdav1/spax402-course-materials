# Week 9 quiz bank (core questions)

<!-- /quiz-me asks these plus one dynamic question generated from your own work.
     No answer key lives in this repo — understanding is the answer key. -->

1. What is the difference between exploratory and explanatory analysis? Which one belongs in
   front of a coach or general manager?

2. Which format gives you the most control over what the audience sees and when, but holds the
   least detail? Which gives the reader the most detail but the least control? Where do a
   narrated video and a dashboard fall?
   <!-- The student should place a live presentation, an emailed report or spreadsheet, a
        narrated video and a dashboard. If they only name two, ask about the other two. -->

3. When is a dashboard a better choice than a narrated video, and when is the video better?
   <!-- Probe both directions: an audience with only a few minutes, and a presenter who wants
        to set the order. Do not give the answer. -->

4. Why is a bar chart usually easier to read than a pie chart? Does that change when the values
   are percentages?

5. What are pre-attentive attributes? Give an example of using one to point a coach at the one
   bar that matters.

6. What is the data-ink ratio trying to maximize? Would bright colors or 3-D effects help it?

7. Your visualization skill made a chart that breaks one of your rules. Why not fix the chart by
   hand, or remind Claude of the rule in the chat? What should you do instead?
