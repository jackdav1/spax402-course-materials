# Week 5 quiz bank (core questions)

<!-- /quiz-me asks these plus one dynamic question generated from your own work.
     No answer key lives in this repo — understanding is the answer key. -->

1. In a regression of second-half wins on first-half wins, say in one sentence each what
   the slope, the intercept and a residual mean.

2. Say the null hypothesis out loud for a regression coefficient, then explain what the
   p-value beside it is the probability of. Use the word "if."

3. Your agent tests twenty predictors of wins and reports the one that came out significant.
   What is wrong with that, and what would you ask it to show you instead?

4. Two statistics correlate at 0.80 across pitchers, and at 0.03 once both are expressed per
   nine innings. Explain what happened, and name the general term for it.

5. A result clears 0.05 with an R squared of one tenth of one percent. How would you describe
   it to a coach, and what would you look at next?

6. Across 178,921 pairs of games, pooling every hitter together, hits in one game correlate
   with hits in the next at 0.027. Comparing each hitter to his own season average, the same
   pairs correlate at 0.005. Both clear 0.05. What was the pooled number measuring, and would
   you tell a manager to use either one?

7. Two coefficients in the same regression are 182 and 0.99. Why can you not compare them
   directly, and what would you do so that you could?
