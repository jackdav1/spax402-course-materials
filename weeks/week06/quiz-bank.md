# Week 6 quiz bank (core questions)

1. You add x² to a regression of points per game on time on ice and its coefficient comes out
   positive. Say in words what that means for the 11th minute against the 21st.

2. A quadratic fit of a player's output on his ice time bottoms out at eight minutes and climbs
   below that. What three things should decide whether you keep the curve, change the degree,
   or change the model?

3. Two combine prospects weigh 165 and 341 pounds. Explain how a regression of 40 time on
   weight lets you say which one is faster for his size, and how you would turn that into a
   number a coach can read.

4. Explain what an interaction term lets a model say that the model without it cannot. If its
   coefficient comes out negative, are the two variables synergistic or antagonistic, and what
   does that say about having both compared with the sum of each alone? Give an example from
   basketball.

5. A variable has four categories. How many dummy variables go into the regression, what is the
   reference category, and what does each dummy's coefficient measure?

6. Players who get more minutes score more per game. Why does that curve not tell a coach what
   would happen if he gave a bench player more minutes?
