# Week 4 quiz bank (core questions)

<!-- /quiz-me asks these plus one dynamic question generated from your own work.
     No answer key lives in this repo — understanding is the answer key. -->

1. Say in one sentence each what a descriptive statistic and a probabilistic statistic do,
   then name one of each from a sport you follow.

2. Points per game is usually filed as a counting statistic even though it has a denominator.
   Explain why, and name the denominator that would turn it into an efficiency statistic.

3. Minnesota made more field goals, took more shots and turned the ball over less than New
   York in Game 3 of the 2024 WNBA Finals, and lost. Say which efficiency statistic explains
   the result and why the counting statistics could not.

4. Dean Oliver's Four Factors are described as exhaustive and mutually exclusive. Say what
   each word means for a set of statistics, and why both properties matter when you want to
   price a tradeoff between two lineups.

5. Slugging percentage and wOBA both weight hits. Say how the weights are chosen in each one,
   and what wOBA counts that slugging leaves out entirely.

6. Two hitters finish within ten points of batting average and more than seventy places
   apart in wOBA. Describe the two stat lines that would produce that.

7. Expected goals is a probability. Name three inputs an xG model uses, and one thing about a
   shot it deliberately ignores. Explain why it ignores it.

8. A team out-shoots its opponent by more than a full expected goal and loses 2 to 0. Say what
   "deserved to win" means in xG terms, and one reason the xG total might not describe how
   the losing team actually played after the first goal.

9. Explain why a coach optimizes for win probability rather than expected point differential,
   using the deep three at the buzzer or the kneel-down as the example.

10. EPA and WPA share five inputs and differ by one. Name the one, then describe a play where
    EPA is large and WPA is small, and a play where the reverse holds.

11. You have a column of EPA and a column of WPA for the same plays. Say what STANDARDIZE does
    to each column, why you need it before comparing them, and what CORREL tells you that the
    z-scores do not.

12. Neither EPA nor WPA is "better." Name one question a coach would answer with EPA and one
    they would answer with WPA, and say why the tool fits the question.
