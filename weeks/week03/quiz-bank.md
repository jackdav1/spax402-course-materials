# Week 3 quiz bank (core questions)

1. A quarter you found on the sidewalk comes up heads five times in a row. The p-value
   against a fair coin is 0.03125. Say in one sentence what that number is the probability
   of, and in a second sentence what it is not the probability of.

2. A QB drafted in the 7th round throws for 5 TDs and 0 INTs in their first game. The fans
   are saying the club found a hall-of-famer. You want to answer how likely that is. What
   would the prior be?

3. Write Bayes' theorem for P(H | D), and label each of the four quantities in it with the
   name the deck used.

4. Same five heads, but you found the coin on the floor of a magic shop. Which of the p-value, the
   likelihoods, the prior and the posterior change, and which stay the same?

5. In Excel, `=NORM.DIST(3.22, 4.66, 0.93, TRUE)` and `=NORM.DIST(3.22, 4.66, 0.93, FALSE)`
   return different numbers. Say what question each one answers about a running back who
   is averaging 3.22 yards a carry against a career 4.66.

6. A hitter with a career .260 average hits .350 in April. His teammate, a career .295
   hitter, hits .350 in the same number of at bats. Whose April is stronger evidence that
   he is now a .300 hitter, and why?

7. A general manager says a hitter's April was "statistically significant" and wants to
   move him up in the order. You have computed the posterior probability that he is a
   .300 hitter at 10%. Explain to the GM how both things can be true at once.

8. You are asked to defend the prior you used for a player. Name two places a prior can
   legitimately come from, and one thing that must not be allowed to set it.

9. In the Thursday hand-build you computed a posterior for Nick Anderson choking. Explain
   why two students with the same likelihoods could both be right and still disagree on
   the posterior, and what they would have to agree on to settle it.

10. Your agent reports a posterior of 92% that a player has changed, from an April of 40
    at bats. What are the first two things you check before you repeat that number to
    anyone?
