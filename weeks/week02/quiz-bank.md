# Week 2 quiz bank (core questions)

<!-- /quiz-me asks these plus one dynamic question generated from your own work.

1. Why can two goalies with identical even-strength and power-play numbers still post
   different overall save percentages?

2. Write the probability that a 61.7% free throw shooter makes at least one of two attempts.
   (Consider calculating the complement rather than adding up the three outcomes that comprise
   your answer.)

3. In Excel, `=BINOM.DIST(8, 10, 0.9, FALSE)` and `=BINOM.DIST(8, 10, 0.9, TRUE)` return
   different numbers. Say what each one answers, and which you would use for "at least 8 of 10."

4. Two hitters finished at .331 and .311 on roughly 550 at bats each, and their 95% intervals
   overlap across most of their width. What can you tell a general manager about which hitter is
   better, and what can you not?

5. A 61.7% free throw shooter has a margin of error of about 9.5 percentage points after 100
   attempts. Roughly how many attempts would you need to cut that margin of error in half, and
   why is the answer not "twice as many"?

6. A kicker, John Smith, has an FG% of 84%. The league made 85% of all field goals, but from
   the distances Smith attempted, a league-average kicker would have made 82%. Do you think he
   is better or worse than a league-average kicker? What else would you want to know?

7. Twenty-seven of thirty-one qualifying kickers have intervals containing the league average.
   A GM tells you they've seen that stat and have determined that "every kicker is the same" and
   not worth spending money on. How would you respond?

8. Several of the intervals you computed run past 100%. Explain what assumption produced an
   impossible number.
